Why parents choose TBD

A school small enough to know every child

We keep classes small so teachers can see how each child is doing, and so parents always have someone to talk to.

A head for every section

Nursery, Lower Basic and Upper Basic each have their own head, so someone always knows your child’s year group and can answer for it.

Two adults in the youngest rooms

Nursery 1 and Nursery 2 have a class assistant working beside the teacher, so the youngest children always have a second pair of hands.

A safe, supervised campus

A compound master and security team cover the grounds through the school day, with first aid in place for every class.

Admissions 2026/2027

Four simple steps to join us

You can do the first three from your phone. Places in each class are limited, so we recommend applying early.

  1. 1

    Download the form

    Get the form for your child’s class from the admissions page, or collect one at the school.

  2. 2

    Fill it in

    Complete every section and gather the documents listed on the form.

  3. 3

    Upload and pay

    Submit the form online and pay the ₦5,000 application fee. No need to travel to the campus.

  4. 4

    Meet us

    We call you to arrange an assessment and a tour of the school.

Visit us before you decide
